📦 PROJECT OVERVIEW
═══════════════════
A premium, production-ready personal portfolio website built with React,
Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion. Designed for AI/ML Engineers and Full Stack
Developers who want to stand out.

🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM
════════════════
Theme:        Premium Royal White
Primary:      Royal Blue    (#1E40AF)
Secondary:    Premium Gold  (#D4AF37)
Accent:       Emerald Green (#10B981)
Background:   White / Pearl / Ivory
Typography:   Inter (body) + Poppins (headings)
